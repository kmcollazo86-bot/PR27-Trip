Good to know

  • No passport needed for US citizens. Bring a valid driver's license or ID.
  • US dollars everywhere, but bring some cash for kioskos, chinchorros and parking.
  • Tolls: most rental cars have an AutoExpreso toll tag. Ask how they bill it at pickup.
  • January weather is warm, with quick showers. Pack a light rain jacket.
